What are Graphene Liquid Cells?

Nano- to microscale pockets of water sealed between graphene layers — the most electron-transparent window for liquid TEM.

Around the pocket, the two graphene layers stick together due to strong static forces, giving rise to a very stable pocket that is both water and vacuum tight.

Samples remain dissolved and hydrated — drying, freezing, or fixating are no longer required.

FAQ

Common questions about graphene liquid cells and the Naiad system.

Is it difficult to prepare a graphene liquid cell (GLC) sample?

Although manually preparing the graphene liquid cell sample can be challenging, VitroTEM provides an easy-to-use automated system for the assembly of graphene liquid cells (GLCs).

What is the resolution of graphene liquid cell (GLC) sample images?

Because of the low thickness of the liquid layer (~100 nm) entrapped between two thin graphene layers, graphene liquid cells for TEM enable high-resolution imaging.

Is a special TEM holder required?

The graphene liquid cell sample can be prepared on a conventional TEM grid and imaged using a conventional TEM holder.

What samples can be imaged?

Graphene liquid cells enable dynamic TEM imaging of whole biological cells as well as single molecules and nanoparticles in liquid at unprecedented resolution.
